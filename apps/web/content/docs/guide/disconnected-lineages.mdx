---
title: Disconnected lineages
description: Families that don't connect yet, people with no recorded ties, and how their order on the page sets their order on the tree.
---

A Kul project doesn't have to describe one connected tree. Research rarely
works that way: you find a great-grandmother's family before you can say
how they connect to yours, or you know of a cousin but not which branch
they belong to.

Write them down anyway. Each group that isn't connected to the others is
drawn as its own tree, side by side with the rest:

```kul
# The Park family
person jinho   name:"Jin-ho Park"   gender:male    born:1960-04-12
person soojin  name:"Soo-jin Park"  gender:female  born:1963-08-25

marriage m_jinho_soojin jinho soojin  start:1988-05-14

person minjun  name:"Min-jun Park"  gender:male  born:1990-07-03
  birth m_jinho_soojin

# Nadia, with no ties recorded yet
person nadia  name:"Nadia Haddad"  gender:female  born:~1985

# The Costa family
person bruno   name:"Bruno Costa"   gender:male    born:1955-02-09
person helena  name:"Helena Costa"  gender:female  born:1958-11-28

marriage m_bruno_helena bruno helena  start:1980-06-21
```

A person with no marriage, birth or adoption is drawn as a single card.
That isn't an error; it's all you know so far.

## Order on the page is order on the tree

The separate trees are placed left to right in the order they appear in
your files: a family where its first marriage is written, a lone person
where their `person` line is. Above, the Parks come first, then Nadia, then
the Costas. To move a family to the left, move its lines up. The same goes for
brothers and sisters inside one family: they appear in the order their
`person` lines are written.

## Connecting them later

When you find the link, add it. If Nadia turns out to be Bruno and Helena's
daughter, give her a `birth` line:

```kul
person bruno   name:"Bruno Costa"   gender:male    born:1955-02-09
person helena  name:"Helena Costa"  gender:female  born:1958-11-28

marriage m_bruno_helena bruno helena  start:1980-06-21

person nadia  name:"Nadia Haddad"  gender:female  born:~1985
  birth m_bruno_helena
```

Nothing else changes. Her card moves into the Costa family, under her
parents' marriage.
