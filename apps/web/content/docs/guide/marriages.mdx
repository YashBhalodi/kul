---
title: Marriages
description: The marriage line, the host and the joining spouse, start and end dates, and why a death does not end a marriage.
---

A marriage joins two people:

```kul
person ravi   name:"Ravi Iyer"   gender:male    born:1949
person meera  name:"Meera Iyer"  gender:female  born:1952

marriage m_ravi_meera ravi meera  start:1976-02-14
```

After `marriage` come three ids: the marriage's own id, then the two
spouses. Like a person's id, the marriage id must be unique in the project.
Children use it to say which marriage they came from, so a name such as
`m_ravi_meera` keeps the file readable.

The two spouses must be two different people, and both must be declared
somewhere in the project. Kul doesn't mind whether a marriage comes before
or after the people in it.

## The host

The order of the spouses matters. The first is the marriage's **host**, and
the second **joins** the host's family. On the tree, the couple sits where
the host belongs, in the host's family. The joining spouse's own birth
family, if you record it, is drawn as a separate tree.

Put first the person whose family you are telling the story of. If Meera's
family is the one you are recording and Ravi married into it, write
`marriage m_meera_ravi meera ravi`. To change the host, swap the two ids.

## Start date

`start:` is when the marriage began. It is optional: wedding dates are often
lost, and a marriage without one is still a marriage. Write it as precisely
as you know it, as in [dates](/docs/guide/dates/).

## Divorce: end and end_reason

A marriage that ended in divorce gets an `end:` date and
`end_reason:divorce`. The two always go together:

```kul
person erik    name:"Erik Lindqvist"    gender:male    born:1970-01-22
person astrid  name:"Astrid Lindqvist"  gender:female  born:1972-10-08

marriage m_erik_astrid erik astrid  start:1995-06-17  end:2003-06-15  end_reason:divorce
```

`divorce` is the only end reason. If you know a marriage ended but not when,
use the closest date you have, such as `end:~2003`.

Leaving one of the pair out is an error, which the editor offers to fix:

```kul expect-error=KUL-R05
person erik    name:"Erik Lindqvist"    gender:male
person astrid  name:"Astrid Lindqvist"  gender:female

marriage m_erik_astrid erik astrid  start:1995-06-17  end:2003-06-15
```

On the tree, an ended marriage is drawn fainter than a current one.

## A death is not a divorce

When a spouse dies, don't end the marriage. Record the death on the person
with `died:`, and leave the marriage as it is:

```kul
person bob    name:"Bob Sharma"    gender:male    born:1948-11-30  died:2020-03-15
person alice  name:"Alice Sharma"  gender:female  born:1950-04-12

marriage m_alice_bob alice bob  start:1972-05-12
```

The marriage is still part of Alice's story: she is Bob's widow, not his
former wife, and she stays in the family she married into. Kul can tell
that the marriage stopped being active when Bob died, from his `died:`
date, so it doesn't need to be told twice. `end:` is only for a marriage
that the two people ended while both were alive.

## The same couple, twice

If two people divorce and later marry again, write two marriages with
different ids. Each child names the marriage they were born into:

```kul
person alice  name:"Alice"  gender:female
person bob    name:"Bob"    gender:male

marriage m_alice_bob_1 alice bob  start:1972-05-12  end:1980-01-01  end_reason:divorce
marriage m_alice_bob_2 alice bob  start:1985-06-15
```

For someone who married different people over their life, see
[multiple marriages](/docs/guide/multiple-marriages/).
