---
title: Where your files live
description: Kul keeps your projects in your browser, on your computer. What that means, how to back them up, and using Kul offline.
---

Kul has no server and no account. Your projects never leave your computer,
which also means nobody else keeps a copy of them. Read this before you
type in a lot of family history.

## In your browser's storage

The app keeps every project in your browser's own storage for this site
(the origin private file system). It is not a folder you can open on your
computer, and other websites can't see it. It belongs to one browser, one
browser profile and one computer: Chrome and Firefox each keep their own, a
second profile starts empty, and nothing is synced between devices.

Saving is automatic, a moment after you stop typing. The top bar shows
**Saving…** and then **Saved**.

## How projects can be lost

Browser storage is durable, but not as durable as a file you keep yourself:

- Clearing your browsing data or this site's data,
  or uninstalling the browser, deletes your projects.
- A browser short of disk space may clear a site's storage to free up space,
  unless it has agreed to keep it. After your first edit, Kul asks the
  browser to keep your projects. Whether it agrees is up to the browser.
- A private or incognito window may keep nothing at all,
  or only until the window closes. If Kul can't save, a banner says so.

Open **Settings** from the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>,
then type "settings") to see how much space your projects use and whether
the browser has agreed to keep them. When it hasn't, the app shows a
reminder with a **Download backup** button.

## Backing up

Download a project as a `.zip` from **Export** › **Download project
(.zip)**. Keep the file somewhere you back up anyway. The archive holds
nothing but your `.kul` files, so it stays readable without Kul.

To restore it, or to move a project to another browser or computer, open
the project menu and choose **Import as a new project** › **Files or a
.zip…**. See [projects, import and export](/docs/app/projects/).

If you keep your family in a folder of `.kul` files instead, under version
control or in a synced folder, import that folder whenever you want to see
it in the app, and use the [command line](/docs/command-line/) to check it.

## Working offline

After your first visit, Kul keeps a copy of the whole site, the app and
these docs included, and loads without a connection. Docs search works
offline too.

When a new version of Kul is published, the app downloads it in the
background and shows "A new version of Kul is ready". Click **Reload** to
switch; your work is saved first. Until you do, you keep the version you
have.
