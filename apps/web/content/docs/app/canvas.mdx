---
title: The canvas
description: Moving around the tree, selecting people and marriages, the details panel, and how the tree and the editor follow each other.
---

The canvas fills the screen behind the editor and shows the family tree.
It redraws as you type. [Reading the tree](/docs/reading-the-tree/) explains
what the cards and lines mean.

## Moving around

Drag the background or scroll to pan. To zoom, hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
(<kbd>⌘</kbd> on a Mac) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad. The buttons in
the bottom-right corner zoom out and in, **Fit** the whole tree on screen,
or show it at its actual size.

When the canvas has the keyboard (click its background), the arrow keys
pan, <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> zoom, and <kbd>0</kbd> fits the tree.

The first time a project draws a tree, Kul fits it to the screen. After
that the view stays where you leave it, and is saved with the project.

## Selecting

Click a card to select that person, or a marriage line to select the
marriage. Three things happen:

- The selection is outlined on the tree.
- The **details panel** opens on the right, with everything recorded about
  them: dates, parents, marriages and children. Click a name in the panel
  to select that person instead.
- The editor shows the line that declares them, without taking the keyboard
  from the canvas.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or close the panel to clear the selection. The
selection stays put while you edit, as long as the person or marriage is
still there.

From the keyboard, <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves through the people's cards and the
marriage lines on the tree, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> selects
the one in focus. From the editor, the command palette's **Show details for
the entity at the cursor** selects the person or marriage your cursor is on,
and leaves the cursor where it is.

## The editor follows you, and you follow the editor

The link works in both directions. Move the cursor in the editor onto a
person or a marriage, and the tree marks it with a softer outline and
brings it into view if it's off screen. Click something on the tree, and
the editor jumps to it.

On a past record (a faded card), the ↺ badge takes you to the person's own
card.

## When the tree can't be drawn

While your files have errors, the canvas keeps the last tree that worked,
dimmed, with a note such as "2 errors — showing the last valid tree". Click
the note to see the problems. If there has never been a tree, the canvas
says what to fix instead.

An empty project shows **Add your first person**, which writes a starter
person into the open file for you to change.

## Saving the picture

**Export** in the top bar has **Download tree (.svg)**. The SVG is a
self-contained drawing of the tree with its legend, in light colours, that
opens in any browser. It is only offered while the project has no
errors.
