---
title: Fields
description: Every field of every statement, whether it is required, and the values it takes.
---

A field is written `name:value`, with no spaces around the colon. Fields
can come in any order, and each may appear once per line. Leave out a field
you don't know.

## person

```text
person <id>  <fields>
```

| Field    | Required | Value                       | Meaning                                     |
| -------- | -------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `name`   | Yes      | Text, in quotes             | The name shown on the person's card.        |
| `gender` | Yes      | `male`, `female` or `other` | The person's gender.                        |
| `family` | No       | Text, in quotes             | The family name, kept apart from `name`.    |
| `given`  | No       | Text, in quotes             | The given name, kept apart from `name`.     |
| `born`   | No       | A date                      | When the person was born.                   |
| `died`   | No       | A date                      | When the person died. Absent: not recorded. |

## birth

```text
  birth <marriage-id>
```

Indented under a person. The person was born to the two spouses of the
named marriage. No fields: the date is the person's `born:`. At most one
per person.

## adoption

```text
  adoption <marriage-id>  <fields>
```

Indented under a person. The person was adopted by the two spouses of the
named marriage. Any number per person, alongside a `birth`.

| Field   | Required | Value  | Meaning                                                   |
| ------- | -------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `start` | No       | A date | When the adoption took effect. Write it when you know it. |
| `end`   | No       | A date | When the adoption ended. Absent: it lasts.                |

## marriage

```text
marriage <id> <host-id> <spouse-id>  <fields>
```

The host is the first spouse; the second joins the host's family. The two
must be different people.

| Field        | Required          | Value     | Meaning                                     |
| ------------ | ----------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `start`      | No                | A date    | When the marriage began.                    |
| `end`        | With `end_reason` | A date    | When the marriage ended in divorce.         |
| `end_reason` | With `end`        | `divorce` | Why it ended. `divorce` is the only reason. |

A spouse's death does not end a marriage: record it with `died:` on the
person.

## Dates

| Form           | Example      | Meaning                             |
| -------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| `YYYY`         | `1975`       | Some day in 1975.                   |
| `YYYY-MM`      | `1975-09`    | Some day in September 1975.         |
| `YYYY-MM-DD`   | `1975-09-03` | That day.                           |
| `~` + any form | `~1975`      | Around then: five years either way. |

Years have four digits, months and days two. The day must exist in that
month and year: `1975-02-29` is an error, `1976-02-29` is fine.

## Text

Text goes in double quotes and can hold any characters, in any script.
Inside the quotes, `\"` is a double quote and `\\` a backslash; a backslash
before anything else is an error.

```kul
person ammu  name:"Asha \"Ammu\" Rao"  gender:female
```
